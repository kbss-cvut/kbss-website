On Friday 13 November 2026 at 11:30 [Martin Ledvinka](https://kbss.felk.cvut.cz/web/team#martin-ledvinka) is going to
talk about the changes in RDF 1.2 and its serializations, and in SPARQL 1.2.
Join the session at [this link](https://meet.jit.si/open-mic-kbss).

##### Abstract

While the RDF 1.2 specification is still in _draft_ state, software libraries like Jena and RDF4J in their latest releases
have already adopted it, so we may consider the changes they bring pretty much a done deal. In this talk we will look
at what exactly has changed, especially in relation to RDF-star, and how the aforementioned libraries support it.
