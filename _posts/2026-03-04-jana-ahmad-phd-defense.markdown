Jana Ahmad spent several years at the KBSS group working on her doctoral research under the supervision of Petr Křemen.
Her research interest included conceptual modeling and optimization of query answering based on data structure.
On March 3, she successfully defended her dissertation thesis titled
[_Towards Queries Optimization Based on Unified Foundational Ontology:A Study in Safety Engineering Domain_](http://hdl.handle.net/10467/127232).

Congratulations, Dr. Ahmad!
