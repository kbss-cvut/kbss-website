On Friday 6th March 2026 at 10:30 [Petr Křemen](https://kbss.felk.cvut.cz/web/team#petr-kemen) -- a former leader of KBSS group -- talks about case for generating event report data for the evaluation of a dashbportability of SPARQL queries

Join the session at [this link](https://meet.jit.si/open-mic-kbss).

##### Abstract
Although SPARQL 1.1. is a W3C recommendation, its gray zones allow SPARQL engines decide on different evaluation semantics. I will discuss several such cases (e.g. DESCRIBE, FILTER/OPTIONAL evaluation) and how SPARQL engines approach their evaluation.
